Who I Am

I'm Baba Osman Bukari — an economist, data analyst, and development advocate. With degrees from Istanbul Commerce University, Ibn Haldun University, and Hamad Bin Khalifa University, I bring a diverse and global perspective to solving economic challenges.

What I Do

My work spans data analysis, economic research, nonprofit accounting, and community development. I've served in roles from research assistant to accountant to project manager, and I’m passionate about using data to inform policy and improve lives.

My Vision

I aspire to contribute meaningfully to development economics and environmental sustainability, particularly in emerging economies. I’m currently preparing for advanced graduate study to further these goals.
